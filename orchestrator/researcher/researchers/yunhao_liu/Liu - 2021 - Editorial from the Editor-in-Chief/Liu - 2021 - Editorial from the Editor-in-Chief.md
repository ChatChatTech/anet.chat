# Editorial from the Editor-in-Chief

In past decades, sensor networks have attracted broad interests from both industry and academia. ACM Transactions on Sensor Networks (TOSN) is the flagship ACM journal in the area of sensor networks. Researchers are willing to share their theory, system, and milestone works in TOSN, and in turn, this high-quality work will attract more people to our research community. My first three-year term as TOSN Editor-in-Chief began in December 2017, and it is my honor to now serve a second term.

Over the past several years, we have witnessed new research trends toward edge/mobile computing, Artificial Intelligence-of-Things (AIoT), and Internet-of-Things (IoT) security/privacy. These topics extend sensor networks’ general goals of a broader scope within the emerging areas of advanced communication technologies, computation platforms, and sensing methods. To further enhance the impact of TOSN in our research community, I am planning to focus on the following four areas:

(1) Clarify the scope. TOSN focuses not only on the wireless sensor networks but also on broad research topics, including AIoT, edge/mobile computing, and IoT security/privacy. As some of these directions have only emerged in recent years, to make the research community fully understand the changes, I will encourage some survey papers and tutorials, as well as primitive research attempts toward these new directions. Moreover, I intend to establish special issues to cover specific topics every year.   
(2) Enhance outreach. I would like to attract more submissions across diverse research areas, for example, by soliciting high-quality papers from prestigious conferences (e.g., Sensys, IPSN, Mobicom, and MobiSys). Moreover, I will extend the approach and scale of TOSN promotion by cooperating with our associate editors (AEs) to invite more high-quality submissions.   
(3) Increase visibility. I would like to cooperate with the program committee of prestigious conferences (e.g., Sensys and IPSN) where selected TOSN papers can be presented. Additionally, I will encourage the authors to expand their conference papers into journal articles.   
(4) Smooth the review process. Our editorial board should be growing continually to refine the speed and quality of review process. To support the increasing number of submissions, I will solicit additional experienced AEs to ensure a smooth review process. Together with our existing AEs, the diverse expertise of these AEs will completely match the extended scope of TOSN.
