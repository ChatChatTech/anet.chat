# Message from Conference Chairs

It is our great pleasure to extend a welcome to all of you who are participating in SERA 2018 (the 16th IEEE/ACIS International Conference on Software Engineering, Research, Management & Applications) being held in Kunming, China on June 13 to 15, 2018. The conference is sponsored by the IEEE Computer Society and the International Association for Computer and Information Sciences (ACIS), and hosted by Yunnan University, China.

The 16th SERA conference has attracted many researchers from all over the world and given the increasing penetration of software into society, it plays a vitally important role in providing a forum for researchers and practitioners from both academia and industry to exchange and debate ideas on all aspects of Software Engineering research, development, management, and applications, particularly at the interface between practice and theory. The conference keynote will be presented by Professor Jixin Ma, The University of Greenwich, UK, Professor Simon Xu, Algoma University, Canada and Professor Keqin Li, State University of New York, USA.

I would like to thank the General Chair, Dr. Roger Lee and Program Co-Chairs Dr. Bing Luo, Dr. Junfeng Wang, and Dr. Zhengtao Yu, and the members of the program committee for their hard work. We hope that you will all enjoy SERA 2018.

Shaowen Yao, Yunnan University, China
